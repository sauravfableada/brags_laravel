Brand Register API

Endpoint

POST /api/v1/brand/register

Content-Type: multipart/form-data

Form Data Parameters

Parameter Name Type Required Description
Step 1: Brand Information
brand_namestringYesName of the brand. Max 255 chars.
brand_logofile (image)YesBrand logo image file (max 2MB).
trademark_officestringYesThe office where the trademark is registered.
trademark_registration_numberstringYesThe trademark registration number.
brand_descriptionstringYesDescription of the brand.
Step 2: Business Information
business_namestringYesName of the business.
business_addressstringYesFull address of the business.
business_contact_emailstring (email)YesContact email (must be unique). Will be used as login.
primary_contact_namestringYesName of the primary contact person.
phone_number_countrystringNoCountry code for phone number.
phone_numberstringNoContact phone number.
website_urlstring (url)NoBusiness website URL.
passwordstringYesPassword for the new user account (min 8 chars).
password_confirmationstringYesMust match the password.
Step 3: Manufacturing & Distribution
manufacturing_locationsstringYesWhere products are manufactured.
distribution_channelsstringYesChannels of distribution.
authorized_resellersstringNoList of authorized resellers.
product_supply_chainstringNoDetails about the supply chain.
Step 4: Product Information
product_categoriesstringYesCategories the products belong to.
products[0][identifier]stringYesProduct ID (EAN, GTIN). At least 1 product (index 0 to 4).
products[0][image]file (image)YesReal life photo of the product.
Step 5: Brand Protection Preferences
sell_under_own_brandboolean (1/0)YesWould you like to sell on Brags under your own Brand Name?
seller_emailstring (email)NoRegistered Seller Email Address.
store_urlstring (url)NoBrags Store URL.
approve_resellersboolean (1/0)YesApprove resellers before they sell?
additional_documentationfile (pdf,doc)NoAdditional documentation (max 5MB).
